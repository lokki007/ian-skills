01 / The problem
Tracking hides in the subject, the body, a link,
a PDF, a phone photo — or all of them.
01 / Today
So a person opens every one, hunts for the number,
and retypes it by hand.
02 / The watchdog
A watchdog reads every email the moment it lands —
text, links, PDFs and photos.
02 / Vision
The AI reads the attachment the way a person would,
and pulls out what matters.
03 / Match
Then it ties the shipment to the manufacturer, PO,
brand, project and city — no lookup needed.
04 / Route
Rules engine applies our rules:
file it, flag it for a person, or leave it alone.
05 / Result
The portal fills itself. People stop sorting —
they just confirm.
